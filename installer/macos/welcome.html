AMPSURD @VERSION@ (public beta)

Free, open-source (AGPLv3) guitar rig for NAM captures: standalone app, VST3 and Audio Unit plugin.

@SIGNING_NOTE@

Installed to:
• /Applications/AMPSURD.app
• /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/AMPSURD.vst3
• /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/AMPSURD.component
